# SaaS & Conversion Copywriting Skill

## 1. Core Philosophy: The Attention Deficit Rule
- **Nobody reads web copy word for word.** Visitors scan, skim, and look for immediate relevance in under 3 seconds.
- **Fluff kills conversion.** If a word or sentence does not clarify value, reduce doubt, or incite action, delete it.
- **Be ruthless with word count.** Cut 40-50% of any draft. Short sentences. Punchy verbs. Active voice.

## 2. Key Frameworks

### A. PAS (Problem — Agitate — Solution)
1. **Problem (Identify the pain):** Name the exact obstacle the user faces (e.g., *"Pourquoi 78% des candidats ratent le 4C2 au TCF Canada ?"*).
2. **Agitate (Reveal the consequence):** Explain why standard methods fail (e.g., *"À cause de l'Expression Écrite. La compréhension est un QCM. Mais l'écrit ne pardonne rien : 60 minutes, 3 tâches, 0 correcteur."*).
3. **Solution (Deliver the antidote):** Present the immediate, concrete fix (e.g., *"Super4C2 vous donne les structures types FEI, le Magic Rewrite C1/C2 et la correction IA en 15 secondes pour sécuriser vos +50 points Entrée Express."*).

### B. The Rule of One
Every page, section, and card must have:
- **One primary target** (Candidate wanting Express Entry CRS +50 bonus).
- **One core promise** (NCLC 7 / C2 guaranteed in all 4 disciplines, especially writing).
- **One desired action** (Start training / calculate CRS points / enter email).

### C. Visual Rhythm & Scanning Patterns
- Use **bold words** on key concepts only (3-4 words per paragraph).
- Keep paragraphs to **1 to 2 sentences maximum**.
- Use contrast badges (`[Problème]`, `[Solution]`, `[+50 Points CRS]`).
- Place numbers and outcomes first (*"60 minutes"*, *"+50 points"*, *"15 secondes"*).

## 3. Checklist for Super4C2 Copy
- [ ] Is it immediately clear why candidates fail (Written Expression)?
- [ ] Does it answer "What's in it for me?" in less than 5 seconds?
- [ ] Are sentences shorter than 15 words?
- [ ] Is there an obvious, frictionless next step (CTA / Email input)?
